# F018 — Policy corpus manifest

One document = one rule that applies to thousands of loans
(DESIGN.md §3.2). This corpus is SMALL and fixed: **66 policy
documents** — 2 versions per loan type × 7 loan types, each version
holding the rule topics that exist for that type. That is tens of
documents, not millions.

Generated by `src/policy/policy_generator.py` (no AWS, no new
dependencies):

```bash
python3 src/policy/policy_generator.py --out ./policy-corpus
```

Output: `policy/<loan_type>/version=<version>/policy_<policy_id>.<ext>`
in all 6 formats (pdf, docx, xlsx, csv, txt, png), plus
`policy/manifest.jsonl` (`record_kind: policy`, with `policy_version`
and `latest` on every row). S3 prefix when uploaded:
`policy/<loan_type>/version=…/` (DESIGN.md §13).

## Versions

| Version | Effective date | Supersedes | Latest |
|---|---|---|---|
| `<type>-v2023-01` | 2023-01-01 | — (first version) | no |
| `<type>-v2024-01` | 2024-01-01 | `<type>-v2023-01` | **yes** |

Applies to all 7 loan types (home, personal, business, auto,
education, gold, msme) — 14 versions in total. A customer record
references the version in force for its loan type in its start year
(`policy_version`, e.g. a 2024 home loan → `home-v2024-01`); a loan
starting before 2023 uses `v2023-01`, one starting after 2024 uses
`v2024-01` until a newer version supersedes it. The version table
lives in `src/policy/versions.py` (shared with the customer
generator); `tests/test_policy_corpus.py` checks the chain.

### What changed between v2023-01 and v2024-01

Nothing in the rule text. Both versions carry the **same sourced
figures**, because only one fetch snapshot of the source material
exists (`data/seeds/terminology.yaml`, fetched 2026-11-12). No
historical figures were invented to make the versions differ. The
versions exist to exercise the versioning mechanism itself:
effective dates, the supersedes chain, the latest flag, and customer
references. When a refresh fetches new figures, add a new version in
`src/policy/versions.py` (e.g. `v2025-01`) and record here what
actually changed.

Rule contents, in brief:

- **Interest rate:** the sourced rate range (`rate_min`–`rate_max`)
  plus the rate basis (e.g. "linked to the repo rate", "linked to the
  1-year MCLR") and the per-lender rate note.
- **Prepayment:** the sourced prepayment sentence for the type.
- **Documents:** the full sourced document checklist for the type.
- **Fees:** the sourced fees and charges list for the type.
- **Tenure:** the sourced tenure bands (up to the max tenure). The
  tenures in `terminology.yaml` carry no per-entry source, so this
  rule is sourced to the same lender pages as the rate ranges for
  that type ("rate sources" above).
- **LTV (gold only):** the RBI gold-directions valuation rule and
  LTV caps (85% up to Rs 2.5 lakh, 80% up to Rs 5 lakh, 75% above
  Rs 5 lakh), from RBI-GOLD-01.

## Missing rules (deliberately omitted)

- **Business and MSME have no prepayment rule.** `terminology.yaml`
  has no prepayment entry for either type, and no prepayment figure
  was fetched from a business/MSME source.
- **Auto has no fees rule.** `terminology.yaml` has no fees list for
  auto (no fee figures were fetched from an auto source).

If a future fetch sources these, add them to `terminology.yaml` and
the rules appear automatically — the generator builds a rule for
every topic it can source and skips the rest.

## Caveats (same as F016)

- The RBI gold directions were read via a page quoting the RBI text
  (TrackLivePrice), not on rbi.org.in directly — see SEEDS.md
  RBI-GOLD-01.
- The CFPB complaint taxonomy is not used for policy rules.

## Why policy docs are not generated at 200k/year (S043)

See DESIGN.md §3.2. In short: one policy rule applies to thousands
of loans, so generating 200,000 copies of the same rule would be
200,000 duplicate documents. Duplicates drown retrieval — a query
about the rate would return thousands of identical policy copies
instead of the one rule and the relevant loan records. Policy
documents are versioned instead: when a rule changes, a new version
is added (tens of documents total) and customer records point at the
version in force. The customer corpus (millions of unique loan
records) and the policy corpus (tens of versioned rules) answer
different questions — "what is the rate on this loan?" vs "what is
the policy?" — and the RAG system needs both.

## AWS follow-up (not built)

Policy docs are generated once, locally, not per-shard in AWS. The
planner/worker upload of the `policy/` prefix (Batch job or a
one-off upload step) is still to be wired — see HANDOVER.md. Do not
reuse the customer record generator for policies: the volume and
versioning are different (DESIGN.md §13).
